Technical Review of Toluene and o-Xylene Distillation Unit

Project Overview

Achieved a top-tier certification score by conducting a comprehensive technical evaluation and simulation of a BTX (Benzene, Toluene, Xylene) fractionation distillation unit using Aspen HYSYS[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). The study focused on optimizing the separation of a 30 ton/hour feed comprising 60% Toluene and 40% o-Xylene at 60°C[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

The thermodynamic framework utilized the Peng-Robinson fluid package, which is highly accurate for non-polar hydrocarbons[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). The simulation methodology progressed systematically from Shortcut Column calculations—to determine the optimal feed stage (Tray 12) and minimum reflux ratio[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)—to a rigorous column simulation (T-100 and T-101) that successfully achieved high purity for both the Toluene distillate and o-Xylene bottom products[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
