STEM Education & Environmental Empowerment

Used Cooking Oil Upcycling Project – Pidie Jaya

Led an independent community empowerment program focused on environmental sustainability during the P3KKN service period in Pidie Jaya. Successfully translated chemical engineering principles into practical, everyday solutions to educate local communities on waste upcycling and environmental stewardship.

1 Product R&D & Formulation

Acted as the sole person-in-charge for formulating liquid soap from household waste cooking oil. Conducted extensive literature reviews and executed iterative laboratory trials to achieve the optimal saponification formula, ensuring the final product was both chemically stable and safe for daily use.

2 Interactive Public Education

Organized hands-on STEM workshops for local elementary students. Demonstrated the chemical conversion process interactively while enforcing basic safety protocols. Additionally, integrated environmental awareness by distributing educational comic books to instill early habits in waste sorting and management.
