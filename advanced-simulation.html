Methodology & Software Utilization

As part of my ongoing Master's degree applied research, I extensively utilize a comprehensive suite of advanced engineering simulation software to solve complex industrial case studies. This multidimensional approach allows for highly accurate predictive modeling across process design, safety engineering, and environmental compliance.

My core technical toolkit includes Ansys CFD and Aspen Plus for rigorous thermodynamic calculations, fluid dynamics visualization, and process flow optimization. To address critical industrial safety parameters, I integrate ALOHA (Areal Locations of Hazardous Atmospheres) software for quantitative risk assessment and chemical hazard dispersion modeling.

Additionally, I employ QUAL2Q frameworks to simulate and monitor river and stream water quality, bridging the gap between chemical plant operations and sustainable environmental engineering.
