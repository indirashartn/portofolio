Preliminary Design of an Ethylene Glycol Plant

From Coal Gasification Route (Capacity: 50,000 Tons/Year)

Project Overview & Execution

Acted as the principal engineer in developing a comprehensive preliminary plant design framework for producing 50,000 tons/year of Ethylene Glycol via coal gasification as a sustainable alternative to conventional petroleum-based routes. The industrial layout was strategically planned for Separi Village, Kutai Kartanegara, utilizing the Mahakam River for vital process water supply.

Taking full technical ownership of the project, I independently formulated and executed rigorous mass and energy balances across all core processing units. This involved managing a 4,587 kg/hour coal feedstock and engineering the entire operational lifecycle—from entrained-bed gasification and syngas purification to catalytic hydrogenation—achieving a final product purity of 99%.

Process Synthesis & Sizing

  • Solely engineered coal preparation units including hammer mills (SR-101) and rotary dryers (DE-101) operating at 200°C.
  • Designed entrained-bed gasification loops operating under extreme parameters of 1,500°C and 40 bar.
  • Calculated utility requirements from scratch, handling a 10,420 kW electricity load and extensive cooling water distribution networks.

Economic Feasibility Analysis

  • Independently structured the economic modeling, achieving a Pay-Out Time (POT) of 3.57 years.
  • Calculated a Break-Even Point (BEP) of 25.71%.
  • Validated project profitability with an Internal Rate of Return (IRR) of 24.01%, strongly surpassing the 18.00% MARR benchmark.
