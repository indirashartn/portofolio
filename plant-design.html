Bachelor's Final Project

Preliminary Design of an Ethylene Glycol Plant from Coal Gasification

Capacity: 50,000 Tons/Year | Grade: Excellent (A)

Executive Summary

This comprehensive project involved the preliminary design and process simulation of an industrial-scale Ethylene Glycol plant, utilizing coal gasification as the primary synthesis pathway. The facility was designed to meet a production capacity of 50,000 tons per year, requiring meticulous thermodynamic evaluation and process flow optimization.

I utilized Aspen Plus extensively to model the entire process system. This involved performing rigorous mass and energy balance calculations, optimizing conversion rates, and designing utility integration systems to maximize overall energy efficiency. Furthermore, a detailed techno-economic analysis was conducted, encompassing capital cost estimation, operational expenditure, and process feasibility evaluation to ensure the plant's commercial viability.

The outcome of this project successfully demonstrated enhanced process efficiency through the strategic optimization of operating conditions and sustainable utility management, ultimately culminating in an "A" grade and subsequent academic publications.
