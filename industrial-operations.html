Industrial Water Treatment System Evaluation

PT Kilang Pertamina Internasional RU II Dumai

Independent Field Observation & Analysis

During my industrial placement, I demonstrated strong proactive initiative by conducting an in-depth independent study of the refinery's water treatment operational modules. I focused heavily on understanding the intricate utility systems required to sustain large-scale refinery operations.

Through extensive field site visits, I systematically verified equipment tags (P&ID mapping) across the core utility processing units. My primary technical focus was on analyzing the filtration units, cation-anion demineralizers, and the deaerator system.

Furthermore, I closely evaluated the demineralizer's complete operational cycle. This included observing the ion-exchange process, analyzing the critical backwash and regeneration phases, and evaluating the effluent management strategy at the designated sump pit where accumulated salt waste was systematically discharged.
