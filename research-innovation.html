PKM-RE Funding Awardee

Ultrasonic Biosorbent Development for Metal Ion Reduction

Exact Research Category | Wastewater Treatment

Executive Summary

Led a highly motivated team to secure competitive national research funding under the Exact Research (PKM-RE) category. The research focused on synthesizing an innovative and sustainable biosorbent to address industrial wastewater contamination.

We implemented ultrasonic methods to enhance the biosorbent's structural properties, specifically targeting the effective reduction of toxic lead (Pb) metal ions. This project encompassed the entire research lifecycle—from initial proposal formulation and laboratory material synthesis to progress monitoring and comprehensive data presentation.
